Scriptlight

Read your script. Look at the lens.

Scriptlight is a camera teleprompter for iPhone and iPad. Your script scrolls past a cue line right below the camera, at your own speaking pace, while you record.

Get Scriptlight on the App Store Free to try. Pro is $29.99, once. No subscription.

Make it stand out • then hit record.

What it does

  • Scrolls at your pace, set in words per minute.
  • Follows your voice: the script scrolls as you speak and waits when you pause (Pro).
  • Captions in your own words, as an .srt file or drawn into the video (Pro).
  • Records with the front or rear camera and the mic you choose, including wireless and USB-C mics.
  • Marks emphasis, breath pauses, sections and notes in your script.
  • Keeps your takes, so you can pick the best one.
  • Writes and tightens scripts with Apple Intelligence, on your iPhone (Pro).
  • No account. Your scripts stay on your device.
